I’ve told dozens of clients in the last year the same uncomfortable truth: the bidding tool was already doing fine. The bottleneck was the landing page. A 30% improvement in LP conversion rate compounds with every dollar of ad spend — way more leverage than the 5–10% lift even the best bidding tool delivers. The tools and tests on this site are organized around that observation.

Why a test database, not just reviews

Most LP tool reviews focus on features. The features don’t matter as much as which specific tests work. The database surfaces the latter: real anonymized A/B tests with the lift each one produced. That’s the underlying signal CRO writers should be using and mostly aren’t.
